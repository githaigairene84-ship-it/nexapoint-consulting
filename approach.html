Our Approach

How NexaPoint Consulting proposes to work with a client, from the first discussion through implementation and evaluation.

About this methodology

Please note: this page sets out NexaPoint Consulting's proposed consulting methods. It describes how we plan to work, and is not a record of completed client engagements.

A four-stage consulting method

Each stage builds on the one before it, so every recommendation is grounded in what we learn and checked against your goals.

  1. Stage 1

    Discover

    Understand the client's business, challenges, goals, and current processes.

    Purpose

    To build a shared, well-evidenced understanding of where the organisation stands today, so that later recommendations solve the right problems.

    Example deliverables

    • Summary of business goals and challenges
    • Overview of current processes and tools
    • Stakeholder conversation notes
    • List of priority issues and open questions

  2. Stage 2

    Strategise

    Analyse findings and develop recommendations aligned with the client's objectives.

    Purpose

    To turn what we have learned into clear, prioritised recommendations that fit the client's objectives, resources and timeline.

    Example deliverables

    • Summary of findings and analysis
    • Prioritised recommendations
    • Proposed roadmap with milestones
    • Agreed success measures

  3. Stage 3

    Implement

    Help organise the implementation of agreed solutions and improvements.

    Purpose

    To help the client put agreed solutions into practice in organised, manageable steps, with clear responsibilities.

    Example deliverables

    • Implementation plan with owners and timelines
    • Process documentation and guides
    • Progress check-in notes
    • Handover notes and guidance for the team

  4. Stage 4

    Improve

    Evaluate outcomes, review performance indicators, and identify opportunities for continued improvement.

    Purpose

    To check what has worked, learn from the results, and decide what to refine next.

    Example deliverables

    • Performance review against agreed indicators
    • Summary of lessons learned
    • Recommendations for further improvement
    • Updated priorities and next steps

Collaboration and communication

A good plan depends on people who understand it and believe in it. This is how we propose to keep you involved from the first conversation to the final review.

  • Agreed ways of working

    At the start we agree who is involved, how we will communicate and how decisions will be made.

  • Regular check-ins

    Progress conversations and short written updates, at a rhythm that suits your team.

  • Plain language

    Findings and recommendations are explained clearly, without unnecessary jargon.

  • Your input shapes the outcome

    Drafts are shared for your feedback before they are finalised.

How progress could be evaluated

Improvement should be something you can see, not just something we say. We propose to agree how progress will be judged before the work begins.

Agree what success looks like

Goals are turned into clear, measurable indicators at the start.

Set a starting point

We record where things stand today, so change can be measured fairly.

Review at milestones

Results are checked at points agreed in the roadmap, not only at the end.

Report and decide next steps

Results are shared plainly, and together we decide what to refine, continue or stop.

Examples of indicators that could be used

  • Time taken to complete key processes
  • Rework or error rates
  • Progress against milestones
  • Adoption of new tools or processes
  • Timeliness and accuracy of reporting

The right indicators depend on each organisation's goals and are agreed together.
