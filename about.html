About NexaPoint Consulting

Who we are, what we believe and how we plan to work with the organisations we serve.

Company overview

NexaPoint Consulting is a management, business strategy and technology consultancy based in Nairobi, Kenya. We help organisations make clearer decisions, run more efficiently and use technology where it genuinely helps.

Many organisations know where they want to go but find it hard to see the most practical route. Our role is to bring structure and clarity to that journey, so that strategy, operations and technology work together rather than pull in different directions.

We are built to support small and medium-sized enterprises, startups and entrepreneurs, established businesses seeking operational improvements, organisations interested in digital transformation, and business leaders who need strategic planning and technology guidance.

The NexaPoint approach

Our consulting follows four connected stages. Each one builds on the last, so recommendations are grounded in what we learn about your business and are reviewed against your goals.

Explore our approach in detail

Four stages

  • Discover: understand your business, challenges, goals and current processes.
  • Strategise: analyse the findings and develop recommendations aligned with your objectives.
  • Implement: help organise the agreed solutions and improvements.
  • Improve: evaluate outcomes and identify opportunities for continued improvement.

Our commitment: practical, collaborative solutions

Good advice is only useful if it can be put to work. This is how we intend to make that happen.

Practical by design

Advice should be something you can act on. We favour clear next steps over lengthy theory.

Collaborative at every stage

Your team knows your business best. We plan to involve you throughout and to share our thinking openly.

Sized to your resources

Recommendations should fit your budget, people and timeline, not a one-size-fits-all template.
