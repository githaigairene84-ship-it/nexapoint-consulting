Our Services

Four core consulting services, each focused on a practical business outcome. Use one on its own, or combine them as your needs change.

  • Business Strategy and Planning
  • Digital Transformation
  • Operations and Process Improvement
  • Data and Business Intelligence

About these services

Please note: these pages describe the services NexaPoint Consulting offers. The deliverables listed are examples of what an engagement could produce. The exact scope is always agreed with the client.

Business Strategy and Planning

We help leaders define where the organisation is going and the most practical route to get there, grounded in a clear view of the market and the business.

What this covers

  • Business goal setting
  • Strategic planning
  • Market and business analysis
  • Growth opportunity assessment
Discuss Business Strategy and Planning

Business challenges we can help with

  • Unclear or competing priorities
  • Growth without a clear plan
  • Uncertainty about which opportunities to pursue
  • Little time to step back and plan

Potential deliverables

  • Documented business goals and priorities
  • A strategic plan with milestones
  • A market and business analysis summary
  • A prioritised list of growth opportunities

Digital Transformation

We help organisations see where technology can add real value, then plan adoption in manageable steps that fit their people and processes.

What this covers

  • Digital readiness assessments
  • Technology adoption planning
  • Workflow digitisation
  • Digital process improvement
Discuss Digital Transformation

Business challenges we can help with

  • Not knowing where to start with technology
  • Manual, paper-based or spreadsheet-heavy workflows
  • Tools that do not work together or are not used
  • Staff who are unsure about change

Potential deliverables

  • A digital readiness report
  • A technology adoption roadmap
  • Designs for digitised workflows
  • Recommendations to improve digital processes

Operations and Process Improvement

We examine how work actually flows, find where time and effort are lost, and recommend practical changes that make good results repeatable.

What this covers

  • Workflow analysis
  • Operational efficiency
  • Process documentation
  • Performance improvement recommendations
Discuss Operations and Process Improvement

Business challenges we can help with

  • Delays, rework and bottlenecks
  • Processes that only exist in people's heads
  • Inconsistent quality or service
  • Rising costs without rising output

Potential deliverables

  • Mapped workflows showing how work happens today
  • Efficiency improvement recommendations
  • Documented standard processes
  • A performance improvement plan

Data and Business Intelligence

We help organisations decide what to measure, plan how to report it, and use that information to make decisions with confidence.

What this covers

  • Business reporting strategy
  • KPI identification
  • Dashboard planning
  • Data-informed decision-making
Discuss Data and Business Intelligence

Business challenges we can help with

  • Information spread across many places, with no single view
  • Uncertainty about which metrics matter
  • Reports that take too long to prepare or go unused
  • Decisions made on instinct alone

Potential deliverables

  • A business reporting strategy
  • Defined KPIs and a measurement approach
  • A dashboard plan and layout
  • Guidance on using data in regular decisions
